The Shahada

One sentence. A lifetime of meaning.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ

Ash-hadu an laa ilaaha ill-Allah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan rasool-Allah

I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His messenger.

FAQ

Questions you may have

What is AllahCall’s dawah mission?

AllahCall is a platform dedicated to sharing the beautiful message of Islam through authentic knowledge and understanding. We provide resources, guidance, and support for both Muslims and those interested in learning about Islam.

Can I ask questions about Islam?

Absolutely. We encourage questions and open dialogue, in live sessions or a private consultation with our scholars.

Are your teachings authentic?

Yes. All our content is based on authentic sources from the Quran and Sunnah, verified by qualified scholars.

How can I support the dawah work?

Share our content, volunteer, donate to our projects, or join our community events. Every contribution helps.
