The Declaration of Faith

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ

I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His messenger.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is AllahCall’s dawah mission?

AllahCall is a platform dedicated to sharing the beautiful message of Islam through authentic knowledge and understanding. We provide resources, guidance, and support for both Muslims and those interested in learning about Islam.

How can I learn about Islam?

Explore our free resources, join our online classes, or schedule a one-on-one session with our qualified scholars.

Do you offer support for new Muslims?

Yes. Beginner courses, mentorship programs, and a welcoming community to help you on your journey.
