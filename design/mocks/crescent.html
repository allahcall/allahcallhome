The crescent logo, on brand

I rebuilt the mark from the purple logo as true geometry (circles for the crescent and swoosh, straight lines for the bars, each fitted to the original's edges), and recoloured it in the site's greens. The three rising strokes with the small marks above read like a stylised “الله” (alif, two lams and the shadda) rising out of the crescent.
