## **Poem**

**When I look at you, I see**

**A person who is like me**

**I see your Expressions & your Tone**

**And feel a bond that makes me known**

> _But are you really my reflection_

> _Or just a projection of my affection_

> _Do I understand as you are_

> _Or only as part of my star_

I see the person I aspire to be

And all the things that set me free

I see myself in all my splendor

And I know that I am not just a pretender

But are you really my reflection

Or just a projection of my affection

Do I understand as you are

Or only as part of my star

I am person with goal and wishes

And I know that life is full of twists

But I will keep going on with all my power

And I will keep glowing bright like a flower

> _But are you really my reflection_

> _Or just a projection of my affection_

> _Do I understand as you are_

> _Or only as part of my star_

Maybe we are both alike

Or maybe we are both distant

Maybe we are both in mirroring

The parts of us we want to bring

> _But are you really my reflection_

> _Or just a projection of my affection_

> _Do I understand as you are_

> _Or only as part of my star_

Self- Mirroring is mystery

A harmony of sameness & difference

A way to relate or to evade

A source of love or indifference

> _But are you really my reflection_

> _Or just a projection of my affection_

> _Do I understand as you are_

> _Or only as part of my star_
